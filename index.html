Synopsis

Born in a small village under the mountains, Luk'yan has given body, mind, and soul to one purpose: to earn his place in the Order of Zorya as a Knight-Paladin. Its teachings have told him what is right, what is wrong, and where he belongs — serving the Seers, walking in holy light, standing firm between folk and evil. Then a day comes when a Seer of the Order arrives into his village, mighty and beautiful, and Luk'yan's heart goes from him all at once. The man is everything he ever dreamed of! Isn't he?
