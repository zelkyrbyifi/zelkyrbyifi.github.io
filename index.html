Synopsis

Raised in a small hunter's village, Luk'yan has spent his whole life preparing – body, mind, and soul — for initiation into the Order of Zorya. Following its teachings, he is certain of what is right, what is wrong, and what his place in the world ought to be: to stand between folk and darkness, guided by the holy light. Then a Seer of the Order visits the village, and he is everything Luk'yan had ever dreamed of. Could such a blessed coincidence be real? Luk'yan is about to find out.
